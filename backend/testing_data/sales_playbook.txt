Title: Google Cloud Customer Success Playbook

1. Engagement Stages:
   - Onboarding → Adoption → Expansion → Renewal.

2. Key Metrics:
   - Product adoption rate must exceed 70% in first 90 days.
   - Customer satisfaction score (CSAT) target: 8.5/10.

3. Escalation Protocol:
   - High-risk customers must be flagged to Customer Success Managers.
   - Engineering escalation only via official JIRA tickets.

4. Renewal Strategy:
   - Begin renewal discussions at least 90 days before contract end.
   - Offer upsell opportunities tied to usage data.
