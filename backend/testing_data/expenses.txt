Title: Expense Reimbursement Guidelines – 2024

1. Travel:
   - Economy class flights only unless approved by VP.
   - Hotel reimbursement capped at $250 per night.

2. Meals:
   - Business meals reimbursable up to $75/person.
   - Alcohol is not reimbursable.

3. Process:
   - Submit expenses within 30 days of the event.
   - Use the internal Concur portal for submissions.

4. Approval:
   - Manager approval required for all expenses > $500.
