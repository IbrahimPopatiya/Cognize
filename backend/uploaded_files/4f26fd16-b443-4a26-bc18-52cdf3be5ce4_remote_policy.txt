Title: Google Remote Work Policy – 2024

1. Eligibility:
   - Full-time employees are eligible for remote work up to 3 days per week.
   - Exceptions may be made for roles requiring physical presence.

2. Security:
   - Employees must connect through the official VPN for all internal systems.
   - Devices must have endpoint protection enabled.

3. Meetings:
   - Teams must schedule at least one in-person sync per quarter.
   - Video conferencing should use Google Meet.

4. Expense Policy:
   - Internet and ergonomic equipment costs can be reimbursed up to $200/month.
